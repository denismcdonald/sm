# Sharal
The Kingdom of Sharal is named after it's current king, a high elf named Sonotar Sharalei who took power 150 years ago when the kingdom was just considered a Duchy. He obtained power through political influence and through negotiations, overthrew the previous arch duchess, A human named Tiffany Clearshroud who would pass away just 10 years later. Since however he has been taking the owlbear by the tufts and spearheaded expansion efforts preferably through political negotiations but in the last 50 years there has been more than a few instances of military occupation. As of right now however there is no active conflict. Sonotar is quite well liked by his people, the extensive expansion of land in such a short time has opened the door to many new settlements from Murin to Hoax as well as an abundance of trade.

On the northern border is the Steelrock mountain range. A name that comes from a special ore that can be found within which is known to dent the pickaxes used to mine them. A place deemed inhabitable due to the rocky terrain and the rumours of dragons that nest within. As of right now, it provides a natural defence against any armies for the kingdom of Sharal.
To the east is the kingdom of Brablemar. One so large it rivals that of Sharal. Established 224 years ago they have gone through many leaders, but have kept the same ideals. A people are as strong as they are together. Which has fostered a massive militia spanning that whole kingdom with self defence and training taught in schools. 

To the south east is a Duchy that goes by Avalari. To call out a Duchy would be like putting a dress on a Cerberus. It is a savage land with a single rule, Strength is influence. The only way to obtain a position of power is to either defeat the previous person of that position in 1v1 combat with spectators or in the unlikely event someone passes from natural causes the most well known student will assume the position in their stead. If in such an event where a student can not be found or already holds a position, The Grand Duchess Amari will name a new successor. She acquired the title Grand Duchess after demanding a title at a recent conference between the 3 nations in which they were shoring up trade routes.

# Europia
Europia - On the eastern edge of the Colles Memorian sits the small settlement of Europia. It was founded just 13 years ago and has a population of less than 100 people. In large part due to the rumours of ghosts appearing on the hills to the east. Some say they have seen the faces of the recently deceased there, while others claim they have seen the faces of those soon to be deceased. Either way, it doesn't exactly make familys want to stick around. Their main export right now is meat. Mainly beef and chicken. With such a little population the locals have expanded lands and has lead to large farming efforts. The town is typically in short supply of cloths and other fabrics and they don't typically have anything in terms of weaponry and armor unless it was scavenged from a fallen soldier or adventurer.


# Hoax
Hoax - Small town turned military outpost 3 years ago to strengthen the eastern border. Those who left either went north to Hoint or made the trip south to Erengrad. Some stayed for personal reasons.
The town used to be a small trade hub between the once 4 territories back when Hoint and Erengrad were an independent duchy. Population varies depending on military presence from as low as 150 to 500 people. The town has no major export currently and imports supplies from the nearby capitals. 
Rumours say that some of the military officers have been a bit too hands on with inspections of merchandise but no direct accusations have been made.

# Introitus
Introitus - founded a bit over 30 years ago at the base of the Steelrock mountain range. The properties of the unique ore attract both scholars and tinkerers who hope to be the one to find an efficient method of harvesting and claim the 1000g bounty from King Sonotar. With a growing population of around 450 people, it is slowly establishing itself as an ideal spot to raise a family thanks to the access of the local school and the close proximity to Hoint. Introitus doesn't have a main export but it does have its hands in a few baskets to help get by with education and the mine being the most obvious ones.
There are a few rumours. Rumour has it the school is run by a tyrannical devil woman - Only children have made this accusation however. Rumour has it the Mayor isn't a fan of King Sonotar and will bad mouth his policies to his advisors. Rumour has it someone in town has come into a large amount of gold and may be looking to skip town. Rumour has it at night a faint chittering sound can be heard from one of the hostel rooms

# Ellesmere
Ellesmere - Home of merchant services. Where gold speaks louder than words, there isn't a thing you can't buy if you have enough coin. Also attracts those hoping to hit it big with the multitude of gambling offered, just don't get caught cheating or you could end up a very sore loser. Brothels, magic items even rare magic components, if you desire it, we have it! Ellesmere boasts a population of over 20,000 people, the 2nd highest in the kingdom. Attracting both those who want to make money quickly or those who already have money looking for unique experiences. It's main export is alcohol, specifically mead and beer which is produced locally. It imports a lot of food from all over the kingdom and generally requires a waiting list or small fee to even enter the city. Rumour has it, well, what Rumours haven't been said about this place. Underground black market, secret warlock rituals, claims of demonic influence keeping people in the city, claims the city harvests emotions from those who stay, claims of a network of slave traders, claims of an underground network offering people the opportunity to kill another person if you can think of a rumour it has probably been lobbied against this city 3 times and counting, and yet, people still come.

# Hoint
Hoint, the city of knowledge. Home of the kingdom's largest libraries and They would argue it's top 5 in the world. Hoint has an independent leadership within the Kingdom of Sharal. Hoint was once considered neutral territory until just 12 years ago when they allied themselves with the kingdom of Sharal. Hoint is massive, with a population of over 100,000 people. Truth is, no one is sure when Hoint was originally constructed. Some say it has always existed, others claim it simply showed up one day. 

With it's access to knowledge it's no surprise the amount of scholars who seek the library and all the secrets that are suspected within. Hoint has taken advantage of its status and has front loaded magic into the development of the city. Magic is freely taught and used like an artform.  Kids will use minor illusion and familiars in games. It's common that at least one person in every 2nd house knows how to cast at least one levelled spell. Magic has been incorporated into all forms of life. Cooking uses magical fire that can't burn cloths, resources like water are created from Magic and distributed continent wide. While many widely praise the bravery to accept Magic with open arms, others have brought up that an over reliance can lead to a complacent lifestyle and become a massive weakness should their Magic begin to fail. 

Hoint's leadership is made up of 2 people, Nalaea, an eladrin elf and Averdyn, a winged elf. Both women have ruled in tandem for over 100 years. 

Rumours:
Some people suspect the mysterious origin of the city means it was a gift by a god or goddess.
Both Nalaea and Averdyn publically deny anything is happening between the two and maintain they view the other as a sister. This has done little to stop any rumours of otherwise however. 
Rumours say they sacrifice their old and sick, using the energy of their souls to fuel the city.
Rumour has it the library contains forms of forbidden knowledge they're trying to hide from the kingdom of Brablemar, and that was the reason for allying with the kingdom of Sharal.
Rumours speaks of a group of spies that have infiltrated the city, but no proof other than faint music playing at night before the skies fill with posters leaking information. 
Rumour has it that there is a baker in town who has the best Apple pies in the world.

# Bantol
South of Pardo lies the often forgotten town of Bantol. Bantol lies within the swampy region of the kingdom and was established to take advantage of the wetlands for crops like rice and bamboo. To call it a town is overselling it. With a population of less than 100 people, all of whom are spread out, there isn't even an established geographical city center. 

As you can imagine, it's not a popular tourist destination nor does the area attract growing families. The population is stagnant with only farmers looking to settle in.

Little else is known about the town. They don't have a public leadership nor any ambassadors. To some, the only proof they have that the town exists, is the rice that they eat.

Rumours. 
Aside from ghost stories about crop monsters and urban legends about bog witches meant to scare kids, there seems to be no rumours from the town.

# Clern
Clern and the Sacred forest.
The exact location of Clern is unknown. Despite being marked on maps, the only way to access it is through the Myclen Grove inside the sacred forest. All witnesses describe being heavily disorientated before arriving at Clern with some describing passing out and waking up there. 

The local driads and druids within the forest forbid anyone from bringing harm to the wildlife and become violently aggressive to anyone who would. 

The mystery of Clern has intrigued both adventures and explorers a like, but very few ever make it. Most famously, a published Explorer, named Grendell Firgrim lead an expedition of 5 people into the forest to find Clern, only for the other 4 members to go missing and arrive back 3 days later in camp. All claiming to have made it to Clern leaving Grendell the only one left out.

The City of Erengrad acts as the main access point into the forest and requires a special permission from the mayor to enter.

Rumours.
Forests, groves and disorientation? You can best believe everyone thinks there are some fey responsible. Claims of seeing pixies are a daily occurrence.
People who enter the forest claim a feeling of being watched at all times. 
Clern's very existence is itself a rumour as no one has come back with sufficient evidence of it's existence besides their first hand recount of the events that all parallel with each other. Large city with lights that communicate with you.
Because of the permission slips required for access, people speculate that the driads have some people in power within Erengrad
